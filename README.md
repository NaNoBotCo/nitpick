# Nitpick · จับทุกป้าย

https://nanobotco.github.io/nitpick/

A demo of reading every sign in a 360 photo: the Mac levels the sphere, cuts 24 full-size views, reads
them with Apple Vision, and sends a model only crops it could not settle, a low-resolution sweep, and
zooms on request. Twelve GoPro Max 2 frames from Mahidol Road, Chiang Mai, 23 Sep 2026, faces pixelated.

- `tool/` — nitpick.py (prep, zoom, cost), vision3.swift, sphere.py, the reader prompt and the routine
- `tools/` — blurred.py (levelled, face-pixelated spheres), assets.py (pictures), build.py (pages)
- `docs/` — the site, English and /th/
